How the holdout was kept clean

  • No overlap: none of the 300 holdout hashes appear in the 509 original training APKs, the 100 added training APKs, or the 100-APK validation split.
  • Threshold chosen on validation, not holdout: 0.7 gave 98% accuracy with 0 benign files flagged on the validation split.
  • Retrain: adding 100 APKs raised validation AUC from 0.9816 to 0.9944.

Earlier pilot

Before the retrain, a 20-APK pilot (10 benign, 10 malware) scored 55% (11/20). It is kept on record in evaluation/FRESH_CORPUS_RESULT.md. It was too small for a stable estimate.

Caveats

  • One 300-APK holdout gives a wide confidence interval. Treat 97% as a result for this corpus, not a general accuracy claim.
