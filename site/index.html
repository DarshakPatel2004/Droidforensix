Method

How the holdout was kept clean

  1. No overlapNone of the 300 holdout hashes appear in the 509 original training APKs, the 100 added training APKs, or the 100-APK validation split.
  2. Threshold chosen on validation0.7 gave 98% accuracy with 0 benign files flagged on the validation split. The holdout was not used for this choice.
  3. Retrain with more dataAdding 100 APKs raised validation AUC from 0.9816 to 0.9944.
