# A2 audit: analysis/ modules (steps 10–21 + support modules + emulate/)

Read-only audit. Repros were run from the scratchpad with `.venv/Scripts/python.exe`. Nothing in the repo was changed.

**Main finding:** most of the steps 10–18 forensics block computes nothing on real pipeline input. Steps 11, 12, 14, 15 and 17 always return empty. Step 10 never uses its entropy or brand signals. So step 18's `zero_day_risk_score` rests almost entirely on the certificate check and the two DEX-name / native-lib heuristics. Unit tests pass only because they feed the inputs the steps expect, not the inputs `pipeline.py` actually passes. Separately, two inline paths can crash the whole pipeline: secrets (H1, confirmed) and step 14 (latent).
