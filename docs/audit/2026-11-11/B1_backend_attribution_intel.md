# B1 audit: backend attribution and threat intel

Slice: `backend/family_id.py`, `backend/threat_intel.py`, `backend/censys_enrichment.py`, `backend/circl_client.py`, `backend/community_intel.py`, `droidforensix_llm.py`.
Read-only. I ran experiments on synthetic inputs only (scratch scripts `b1_sig.py` and `b1_flow.py` in this folder). No network calls were made.

Severity counts: critical 0, high 5, medium 15 (including the B1-33 test-gap), low 13. That is 33 findings, plus 3 already-known items confirmed.
